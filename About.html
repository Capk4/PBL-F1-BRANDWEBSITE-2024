	The North Face was founded in 1966 by Douglas and Susie Tompkins as
a small mountaineering retail store in San Francisco. The name was inspired
by the coldest, most challenging side of a mountain in the northern hemisphere.
Shortly after its founding, the brand shifted from simply selling outdoor gear to
manufacturing its own products, specializing in high-performance equipment
for climbers and adventurers. By the 1970s, The North Face was known for its
innovative outdoor gear, including sleeping bags, tents, and down jackets, which
gained popularity with mountaineers and explorers.

	In the 1980s, The North Face expanded into clothing with a focus on
durable and high-performance outerwear. Their Gore-Tex® jackets became a
signature item for those braving extreme outdoor conditions. By the 1990s,
The North Face's appeal had spread beyond outdoor enthusiasts to urban
fashion, particularly with the popularity of it's iconic Nuptse jacket.

	In 2000, The North Face was acquired by VF Corporation, which helped
propel it into a global brand. Over the years, The North Face has also embraced
sustainability, using recycled materials and launching initiatives like
"Clothes the Loop" to encourage the recycling of old gear. Today,
The North Face is a world-renowned brand, known for it's durability, quality, and
								    							   	commitment to performance and environmental responsibility.

Meet Our Athletes

Trail Runner

Jon Albon

Jon Albon began running at 20 to stay fit during his London commute. Over time, he competed in various race types, from obstacle to trail running. After moving to Norway, he became a full-time athlete, achieving international victories across multiple disciplines. Jon developed a deep connection with nature, excelling in alpine sports like climbing and ski mountaineering alongside running.

Key achievements:

  • Trail World Championships gold in 2019
  • Obstacle Racing World Championships gold from 2014 to 2020
  • Extreme World Series gold in 2017

Trail Runner

Jazmine Lowther

Jazmine Lowther is a trail runner who transitioned from alpine climbing to ultra-running after winning her first 50 km race with no training. She quickly rose in the sport, breaking the course record at the Canyons 100 km. Along with running, she enjoys other endurance sports and is passionate about conservation and climate activism.

Key achievements:

  • 4th, CCC 100 km, UTMB World Series Finale, 2022
  • 1st, Canyons Endurance Run 100 km, 2022 (course record)
  • 3rd, Chuckanut 50 km, 2022

Trail Runner

Germaine Grainger

Germaine Grainger, based in the Maritime Alps of southern France, is a trail runner who found his passion for ultrarunning after shifting from competitive cycling in his twenties. Living in the mountains, he saw running as the easiest way to navigate challenging terrain. A former geologist, he finds deeper meaning in his runs by connecting with the history of the mountains.

Key achievements:

  • 1st, 90k Mont-Blanc, 2023
  • 2nd, Diagonale des Fous, 2023
  • 1st, Val d’Aran by UTMB, 2022
